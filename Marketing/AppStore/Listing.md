## Description
Buy the dip. Calmly. No account, no login: open the app and start.

Dipwise turns "I'd buy it if it dropped" into a plan that watches itself. Draw the price range where you'd happily buy, and the range where you'd take profit. Dippy, your stock-watching owl, keeps an eye on the market day and night and taps you the moment a stock gets there.

DRAW YOUR ZONES
• Drag two handles on a real chart to set a buy zone or a sell zone
• See where the crowd plans to buy, right on the same chart
• Know your odds: how likely each plan is to trigger in the next 30 or 90 days

NEVER MISS YOUR MOMENT
• An alert the minute a stock enters your zone, pushed from Dipwise's servers so it never waits for your iPhone to check
• A gentle heads-up when a stock gets close
• Every plan in one place: buy zone, sell zone, and smart alerts set together

28 SMART ALERTS, IN PLAIN ENGLISH
• Sharp drops, new 52-week lows, and big daily moves
• Moving averages, golden crosses, and death crosses
• RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands
• Trailing stops, stop losses, profit targets, and back-to-break-even
• Dividend alerts
Every alert comes with a one-line explanation from Dippy, so you always know what it does.

YOUR PORTFOLIO, IN ONE CALM PLACE
• Import positions from Robinhood, Fidelity, Charles Schwab, Vanguard, E*TRADE, Interactive Brokers, Trading 212, DEGIRO, or any spreadsheet
• Value, today's move, total return, and dividend income in your own currency
• Compare your portfolio with any ETF, index, stock, or crypto over any period (Pro)
• Your Profile: your investor DNA scored against the flock, the blind spots brokers watch for, and how often you go against the crowd (Pro)
• Passive income planner: set a monthly income goal and see the year you reach it and what to invest each month (Pro)
• Your Odds: a cone of where the price could go and the chance it reaches your buy or sell zone within 30 and 90 days, with the typical wait and the likely price range a month out (30-day buy odds free, everything else Pro)
• Flock alerts: when a stock enters the crowd's buy zone, the flock starts buying, or a top investor adds it (Pro)
• A watchlist with prices, trends, and how close each plan is

LIVING WIDGETS AND APPLE WATCH
• Six widgets, each with Dippy in the same corner: Portfolio, Your Plans (how far each stock is from your zone), Stock Plan, Flock Buy Zones (where every Dipwise investor plans to buy), Flock Mood, and Market (open or closed, with a countdown to the bell)
• Optimized for iPhone Duo: stand it on your desk and the outer display becomes a live stock tracker, edge to edge: the big indexes, your portfolio with a chart you can scrub, a heatmap of your stocks, your closest plans, and the flock's fear and greed. Tap any stock to zoom in
• On Apple Watch: your portfolio, closest plans, and charts you scrub with the Digital Crown, plus complications for your portfolio and market hours

A ONE-MINUTE DAILY HABIT
• A short daily brief: how your stocks moved, your closest plans, and how you feel about the market, answered right back with the whole flock's anonymous fear and greed
• Streaks and achievements that unlock outfits for Dippy, worn everywhere he appears

## Creative assets: product page header and search results (`Header/`, `Search/`)

**Header: Dippy's forest.** A flat illustration in the icon's own style: the icon's navy sky with its faint lines, and firs scattered at different depths, kept low. Dippy rises large from the bottom edge, so his face and big eyes are the first thing anyone sees. Above him is one line, "Your stock-watching owl.", whose "o" is the full moon. Every language uses the moon as a letter: the "o" of the word for owl, the ö of Börse with its dots kept, or the full stop in Japanese and Korean. The top stays quiet sky, where iPhone puts the Dynamic Island and the back and share buttons.
- **Safe area:** iPhone shows only the middle 3:2 of the 16:9 header, with the Dynamic Island and the back and share buttons along the top. Dippy and the moon sit inside that area, below the top band.
- **Search results:** the header's sibling. It has the same night forest and the same big one-line message with the moon as a letter ("Get alerted when st●cks dip."). A real iPhone rises from the bottom, with the buy-zone alert lifted off its lock screen, large enough to read as a small search result, and Dippy perched on top. Everything, including the lock screen date and the alert, is drawn in each of the 8 languages. This follows Apple's search guidance ("state the obvious", "show the firsthand experience") and what published A/B tests show works: one short, bold line and one clear focal point.
- No pricing, URLs, ©, awards, or other platforms. No transparency, and the exact sizes App Store Connect accepts.

**Header video (`HeaderVideo/<lang>/header-3840x1646.mov`):** a seamless 12-second loop at App Store Connect's header video spec: 21:9, 3840 x 1646, 60 fps, H.264, no sound. The moon stays in the "o" the whole time and breathes softly while the stars twinkle. Dippy blinks, looks up at the moon (which brightens while he watches), and winks at you. The first frame is the poster, saved as `poster-3840x1646.png`. Every move is eased, and Dippy's faces blend into each other. **Day and night: `HeaderVideo/<lang>/header-day-night-3840x1646.mov`, in all 8 languages.** A real day and night in 20 seconds at 30 fps (each frame blends two moments for a touch of motion blur), as one continuous cycle. The sun crosses the sky on one arc at an even pace: it rises behind the firs on the right and sets behind those on the left. The sky follows it: the icon's teal by day, then a long dusk from the sun nearing the trees until the moon is well up, the icon's navy at night, and a long dawn around sunrise. The full moon rises as the sun sets. Its arc peaks exactly at the "o" of "owl", where it slows and covers the letter, a real "o" in the same font as the rest. Then it sets in the forest left of Dippy, well before dawn, keeping the same size and color all the way. Dippy follows the sun and the moon and winks; he never sleeps. Made by `Marketing/ScreenshotStudio/header_daynight.py`.

**How the App Store crops the 21:9 header (and its videos), measured from previews:**
- **iPhone** shows only the middle, about 1.5 times as wide as it is tall. The back and share buttons sit at the top corners, below the Dynamic Island.
- **iPad** shows the full width but only the middle ~70% of the height. Its tab bar sits at the top center, with back and share in the corners.

The line therefore sits in the iPhone's middle, below its buttons and below iPad's tab bar. Dippy sits high enough that iPad still shows his eyes and beak. The 16:9 still keeps its iPhone-tuned layout.

**Before uploading,** run `swift Marketing/ScreenshotStudio/validate_assets.swift Marketing/AppStore`. It checks every header and search image and every header video against App Store Connect's specifications (sizes, formats, alpha, frame rate, length, codec, tracks). Header videos go only in the **21:9 product page header** slot. The 16:9 slot takes only 5244 x 2950 images, and the search results slot takes 3:2 or 16:9 images, or 3:2 video.
