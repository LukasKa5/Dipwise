# Dipwise Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Dipwise is built so your money stays your business. There are no ads, no trackers, and no data sales.

## What stays on your device
Your stocks, shares, average costs, cash, alerts, notes, journal, streaks, and settings are stored on your iPhone and shared only with your own Apple Watch and widgets. If you sign in with Apple, your name and email stay on your device and are used only to greet you.

## What the Flock shares, if you allow it
With "Share my alerts anonymously" on, Dipwise shares each alert's ticker, type, and price levels. With "Share my portfolio mix anonymously" on, it shares the percentage each stock makes up of your portfolio, and when you add to or trim a position. Both are stored in Apple's CloudKit public database under a random identifier created on your device. They never include your name, email, amounts, share counts, prices you paid, or notes. Crowd statistics are only shown once enough members contribute that no individual can be singled out. Turn either option off in Settings to remove that data.

## Market data
To show prices, Dipwise requests quotes for the tickers you follow from a market data provider. Those requests contain ticker symbols only.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Dipwise never sees your payment details.

## No tracking
Dipwise does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers, and does not include third-party analytics.

## Your choices
You can stop sharing with the Flock at any time in Settings, which removes your shared data. Deleting the app deletes everything stored on your device.

## Not advice
Dipwise is not a broker and does not give investment advice. Odds, crowd statistics, and signals are information, not recommendations.
